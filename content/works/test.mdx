# Aware initial research and branding — Pritanjan Shome
Aware UI Colour Exploration and Branding 2018
---------------------------------------------

### Aware was launched as a proof of concept, we conducted a weekend hackathon to design and develop the initial prototype of the aware product and we stuck with the product while zoojoobe was in the process of being acquired.

### The initial designs looked like this:

![Aware-Old.png](https://images.squarespace-cdn.com/content/v1/5c84b220840b16e5e41e54c3/1552384922122-NZZH45FOQPCSM8MXFMBS/Aware-Old.png)

### **Well thats not too good looking**

### This design looks dated but did give us proof of concept and helped us understand a market fit. We also A-B tested a bunch of combinations and documented the voices that might work and after gathering these insights went on with a baritone male voice.  
These designs have no cohesion when it came to colors and a brand story. Also this was a quick High-level prototype, whose purpose was only and only to test the concept.

### **The Process**

### We started with a collection of what emotions users of aware and people working on aware would associate with a mindfulness meditation app. we asked them to share "5 - 10 adjectives that you associate with Mindfulness meditation" via a google form and gathered 14 responses.

![Screen response sheet](https://images.squarespace-cdn.com/content/v1/5c84b220840b16e5e41e54c3/1552374555658-ETA97BMG8WLOSAEMGS6M/Screenshot+2019-03-12+at+11.42.51+AM.png)

![IMG_3431 copy 2.jpg](https://images.squarespace-cdn.com/content/v1/5c84b220840b16e5e41e54c3/1552375059149-M56NDFSKCE2YV7RKCKO8/IMG_3431+copy+2.jpg)

After collecting the responses we noticed a few words that kept popping up:

> Peace, Calm, Happy, Energy, Focus, Minimal, Warmth, Environment.

The Moodboard
-------------

### Based on these keywords I went on the Dribbble and searched, the results we bore from these results were collected as a mood board for further analysis.

### Here is the mood board:

![export_aware-logo-moodboard-1H3AnI.png](https://images.squarespace-cdn.com/content/v1/5c84b220840b16e5e41e54c3/1552375314561-S62TSOZC2ASK7IG5OE19/export_aware-logo-moodboard-1H3AnI.png)

### There were obvious colors and shapes that emerged from this exercise. To remove my biases from the process, i turned this into a low poly pixelation of the entire board.

### you can now notice the color clusters that form. Making a note of these clusters I gathered them into a sheet. These colors now represent a certain emotion and choosing the primary and secondary color were the two most abundant color from the low-poly diagram.

![Image 1.jpg](https://images.squarespace-cdn.com/content/v1/5c84b220840b16e5e41e54c3/1552375574032-LN7XIDLMXREL3Y3K1SFV/Image+1.jpg)

### The color representations were aptly named based on the keywords we choose to represent the emotions/ adjectives aware brand with. and put into the first page of our design documentation.

![](https://images.squarespace-cdn.com/content/v1/5c84b220840b16e5e41e54c3/1552375959269-AC05EOPT9AQF8FMLJ04H/image-asset.png)

### **What is Mindfulness?**

> ### A mental state achieved by focusing one's awareness on the present moment, while calmly acknowledging and accepting one's feelings, thoughts, and bodily sensations, used as a therapeutic technique.

![Circles.png](https://images.squarespace-cdn.com/content/v1/5c84b220840b16e5e41e54c3/1552381334254-R64M35G1VDFMXA5C9KYA/Circles.png)

### **Applying this concept to a word mark**

### Traditionally the Glow around your head also called a Halo or 'the crown chakra' is used to demarcate enlightenment. Circles also mean Common meanings: eternity, female, universe, magic, mystery. We wanted a logo that was circular and clean, also clearly readable.
